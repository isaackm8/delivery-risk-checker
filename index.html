Your file is read in this browser and never uploaded. To check the weather, only the approximate locations (ZIP code centers) of destinations and warehouses are sent to the National Weather Service.

Estimates use straight-line distance between ZIP codes and typical UPS transit times, skipping weekends and major UPS holidays. They are not UPS's official transit times. Weather alerts and snow forecasts come from the National Weather Service, as of when the file is loaded. Orders are judged as of today; "delivered" is assumed from dates, not tracking.
